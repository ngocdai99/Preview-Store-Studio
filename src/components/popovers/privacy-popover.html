Store Privacy & Data Safety Guidelines

1. Questionnaires on Store Consoles: Privacy disclosures cannot be freely edited as text. Developers must complete compliance questionnaires in App Store Connect and Google Play Console.

2. Apple Nutrition Labels: Discloses whether collected data is Linked to You, Not Linked, or used for Tracking.

3. Google Play Data Safety: Discloses third-party data sharing, encryption in transit, and account deletion options.
