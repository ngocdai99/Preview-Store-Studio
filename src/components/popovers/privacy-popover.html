Store Privacy & Data Safety Guidelines

1. Questionnaires on Store Consoles: Neither Apple nor Google permits free-text editing for privacy labels. Developers must complete structured technical compliance questionnaires inside App Store Connect (App Privacy) and Google Play Console (Data safety section).

2. Apple Nutrition Labels: Declares whether collected data (Location, Contact Info, User Content, Identifiers, Diagnostics) is Linked to You, Not Linked, or used for Third-Party Tracking.

3. Google Play Data Safety: Discloses mandatory protocols: whether data is shared with third parties, if data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and whether user account/data deletion mechanisms are supported.

4. Preview Studio Sync: Realistic privacy declarations are pre-configured and automatically synchronized across mockups according to each active Preset (e.g. TaskFlow, PulseFit, ZenMind).
