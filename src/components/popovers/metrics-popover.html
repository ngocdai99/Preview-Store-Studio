Mockup Metrics & Badges Guidelines

1. Not Storefront Listing Fields: Ratings, review counts, category chart ranks, download volume tiers (50K+), age ratings, and in-app purchase tags are dynamically calculated by Apple and Google store algorithms or generated from internal developer questionnaires. They cannot be edited or submitted as listing metadata.

2. Automatic Preset Synchronization: Preview Studio automatically assigns cohesive, realistic illustrative metrics matching each active preset (TaskFlow, PulseFit, ZenMind, CyberStrike, SkillSphere) to ensure device mockups look authentic and production-ready.

3. Clean Day-One & Client Presentation: Use the "Hide illustrative metrics" switch to simulate a newly launched app with zero reviews, or enable "Add Illustrative mockup caption" when sharing asset exports with stakeholders to prevent misrepresenting sample numbers as live store telemetry.

4. Optional Customization: Expand the "Customize Sample Values" section below if you need specific numbers for marketing pitch decks or client design previews.
