iOS App Icon & Xcode Guidelines

1. Xcode Packaging: Must be bundled inside Assets.xcassets (AppIcon) in your Xcode project before archiving.

2. Dimensions & Color: Exactly 1024 × 1024 px (1:1 square) in sRGB or Display P3.

3. No Transparency: Must be 100% opaque without alpha channel (transparency causes upload rejection).

4. Square Corners: Upload sharp square corners. iOS automatically applies the squircle mask (do not pre-round).

5. Design Standards: Avoid promotional text (Free, #1), device frames, or UI elements.
