Why isn't Feature Graphic shown on the Store?

1. Required Console Asset: Google Play Console mandates a 1024×500 px graphic (PNG/JPEG without alpha) before an app listing can be submitted or approved.

2. Store Listing Detail Page: Google removed the standalone banner from phone and desktop store listings to prioritize the App Icon, CTAs, and Screenshots directly.

3. YouTube Promo Video: If you provide a promo video URL, the Feature Graphic acts as the video cover thumbnail with a large play button.

4. Where It Actually Appears: Featured collections, Google Play Home / For You editorial banners, category recommendations, search ads, and Google App Campaigns.

iOS App Icon & Xcode Guidelines

1. Packaged via Xcode: Since iOS 11+, standalone icon upload on App Store Connect is disabled. The 1024×1024 px icon must be bundled inside Assets.xcassets (AppIcon) within your Xcode project before archiving.

2. Exact Dimensions: Exactly 1024 × 1024 px (1:1 square aspect ratio). Color space must be sRGB or Display P3.

3. No Transparency (No Alpha Channel): Must be 100% opaque without an alpha channel. Uploads with RGBA / transparency will be rejected immediately by Xcode & App Store validation (Asset validation failed).

4. Keep Square (No Rounded Corners): Deliver a sharp, square image. iOS and the App Store automatically apply the continuous-curvature squircle mask. Pre-rounding will result in black corner artifacts.

5. HIG Design Standards: Do not include promotional text (Free, Sale, #1), device mockups, or system UI elements. The store icon must match the home screen icon after installation.

Note: These guidelines reflect current Apple & Xcode policies and may be adjusted or changed by Apple in future releases.
