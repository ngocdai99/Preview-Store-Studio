# Store Preview Studio · Plus Edition
> Standalone offline App Store & Google Play preview and metadata studio.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start
1. Double-click **`store_preview_studio.html`** to open in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
2. **Zero installation** — No Node.js, npm, or terminal required.
3. **Private & Client-Side** — Runs locally in your browser with zero tracking, zero telemetry, and no cloud accounts required.
4. **Using Presets** — Switch between 6 built-in templates directly from the top **Preset** menu, or import/export `.json` files from the `presets/` folder to save and restore your projects.

## ⚡ Core Capabilities
* **Dual-Store Simulation**: Apple App Store & Google Play Store across Phone & Tablet viewports.
* **Device Form Factors**: iPhone, iPad, Android Phone, 7" & 10" Tablets (Light & Dark OS themes).
* **View Modes**: Product Detail page, Search Results feed, and Dual-phone side-by-side comparison.
* **Media Formatting**: Adaptive portrait and 16:9 widescreen landscape screenshot carousels.
* **Compliance Checks**: Icon transparency scanner with 1-click auto-flatten to opaque, screenshot dimension validator, emoji-aware character counters.
* **Export Options**: Ultra HD 4K mockups (PNG), complete ZIP bundle, `LISTING_METADATA.md` developer handoff report.
* **Data Tools**: Import/export JSON workspace presets, 1-click Live App Store Cloner.
