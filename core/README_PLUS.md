# Store Preview Studio · Plus Edition
> Standalone, 100% offline App Store & Google Play preview and metadata studio.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start
1. Double-click **`store_preview_studio.html`** to open in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Brave, Edge, Arc).
2. **Zero installation** — No Node.js, npm, or terminal required.
3. **100% Private & Offline** — Runs entirely in your browser with zero network calls.

## ⚡ Core Capabilities
* **Dual-Store Simulation**: Apple App Store & Google Play Store.
* **Device Form Factors**: iPhone, iPad, Android Phone, 7" & 10" Tablets.
* **View Modes**: Dual-phone side-by-side comparison, search feed, portrait & landscape.
* **Compliance Checks**: Icon alpha transparency scanner (ITMS-90717), dimension checks, strict character counters (`Intl.Segmenter`).
* **Export Options**: Ultra HD 4K mockups (PNG), complete ZIP bundle, `LISTING_METADATA.md`.
* **Data Tools**: Import/export JSON presets, live App Store cloner.
